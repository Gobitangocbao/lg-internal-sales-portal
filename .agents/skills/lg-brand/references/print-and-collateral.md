# Print, POP and collateral

## Colour behaviour in print

Active Red's recommended print build is **C0 M97 Y95 K0** (Pantone 2034 C). LG has tested it and
found no colour variation across coated, uncoated, newsprint, eco-friendly paper and PET, and
little difference between the PMS and the CMYK build. Papers named in the test: Vent Nouveau
(uncoated), Recyco (eco-friendly), CCP (coated), newsprint, PET.

For **other materials and silk-screen printing**, the guideline requires making a sample and
verifying it visually before committing — those have not been tested.

For digital display, Active Red `#FD312E` (R253 G49 B46) is developed on **sRGB**, verified on
gram and Mac with display and file colour set to sRGB. Displays cannot be made uniform because of
colour-temperature and white-balance variation, so the value may need adjusting to the actual
display. This is also why the guideline warns against extreme display settings that misrepresent
Active Red.

**Practical rule:** set the document to sRGB for digital and to the CMYK build for print, and for
anything large-format or on an untested substrate, recommend a press proof rather than promising
the colour will match.

## Exhibition, POP and spec boards

Source: `06_Exhibition_Playbook_v1.1.pdf` (V1, 2024).

Overall tone and manner for materials and colour: **Warm, Bold and Simple** — and specifically
avoid using too many colours.

**Logo signage** must be the first thing seen on entering, and sits at the top of every space
inside and outside the hall.

**Spec board / POP, wall type:**

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Board colour | LG Active Red paint, **PMS 2034 C** |
| Product name | cap height **6 mm**, LG EI Headline **27 pt** |
| Model / secondary line | cap height **3.5 mm**, LG EI Text **14 pt** |

**Information / zone wall graphics:** zone titles in EI Headline; highlight title height **160 mm**
on a **3,000 mm** wall in the example given. Keep the information hierarchy consistent across the
whole booth. For island-type displays the information graphic may sit slightly lower than
wall-applied graphics.

**Eye level reference: 1,550 mm** — used for positioning spec information relative to products.

**Lounge and circulation minimums:**

| Space | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Entrance | 1,000 mm |
| Passage | 700 mm |
| Hallway | 1,000 mm |
| Wall | Warm white |

Example dimensions shown alongside: 1,200 mm and 1,800 mm.

**Slogan in immersive content** (TV, LED, projection): follow the video slogan sizing in
`logo-and-slogan.md`; choose the version to suit the display.

## Business card

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Size | 90 × 50 mm |
| Symbol mark colour | Pantone 207 C |
| LG Gray | Pantone Cool Gray 11 C |
| Paper | Hansol Insper Eggshell 300 g, Extra White (eco-friendly) |
| Printing | Offset, **UV ink** — soy ink can smudge the image colour |
| Name | LG EI Text **Bold 10.2 pt**, tracking 0 (EN: −25), horizontal scale 100% |
| Information | LG EI Text **Regular 6.8 pt**, tracking 0, leading 10–11 pt, horizontal scale 100% |

The back carries one of eight graphics: Youth 1, Youth 2, World, Trust, Technology 1, Technology 2,
Human, or the Life's Good slogan in red or white. Epoxy on the back is an option, to lift the
"Future Face" and "Life's Good" graphics.

The symbol mark may appear **without** the logotype on a business card — this is one of the named
exceptions to the symbol-alone prohibition.

## Creative process

The guideline requires adherence to a creative production process, with legal pre-screening,
RHQ/HQ pre-screening and ad pre-testing before release. Joint branding or joint-marketing logo
placement requires prior consultation with GMG. Worth flagging to the user on anything
external-facing: producing correct artwork is not the same as being cleared to publish it.
